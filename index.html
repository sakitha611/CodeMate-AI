Questions teams ask first

Can’t find your answer? Reach us at the contact link below.

Does BugZero store my code?

Your code is used only to produce the review and is not used to train models. Enterprise plans can run inside your own infrastructure.

Which languages does it support?

Python, TypeScript, JavaScript, Java, Go and Rust work best. Other common languages are supported with less depth.

Will it replace human reviewers?

No. It catches routine problems early so human reviewers can focus on design and intent. Nothing merges without your approval.

How long does setup take?

Connecting a repository takes a few minutes, and the first review runs on your next pull request.

Can I change what it checks?

Yes. Team and Enterprise plans let you set review rules, ignore paths and choose which severities are posted.
